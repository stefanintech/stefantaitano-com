Update on the photo frame: it's ALIVE. I rooted it and turned it into a little family hub, all in one evening with Grok Bot.

It shows the family calendar and the weather. Each kid gets their own checklist page. One has a dinosaur egg that hatches when the list is done, and the other has a vampire theme with a Spanish riddle of the day (it turns into a unicorn garden in November). At night it dims and rotates through NASA's Astronomy Picture of the Day.

Honestly it was a blast to build.

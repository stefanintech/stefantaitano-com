Names are locked in for World of Warcraft: Forever. 🎮 Early name reservation runs Oct 27 through Nov 3, and you get three characters, so I used all three before launch on Nov 4.

My main is a priest, and that's because I'd love to get into group content down the road. My alts are a hunter and a mage, all on the PvP ruleset. If I ever just feel like exploring the world, I think hunter or mage would be my second pick.

I'm pretty new to WoW, so I'm going in mostly blind and really just for fun. Not totally blind, though. I played Mists of Pandaria when it came out and got to about level 20, and at the end of the day that's the extent of my WoW experience.

Landed in Alaska for the week, and I already found two spots I'd tell anyone to try. At [Moose's Tooth](https://www.moosestooth.net/) in Anchorage, I had the Elf on the Shelf pizza and the Diablo breadsticks, and both were so good. Then about 40 minutes south, [Crepes de Paris](https://girdwood.com/information/crepes-de-paris/) was parked next to [Girdwood Brewing Company](https://www.girdwoodbrewing.com/), and the berry cheesecake and graham crepe was really, really awesome. Folks up here told me these might be Alaska's best, and honestly, I believe them. If you're ever up this way, go check them out.
